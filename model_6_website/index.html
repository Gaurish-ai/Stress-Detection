Electrodermal Activity (EDA / GSR) -- µS

Measures skin conductance response and sympathetic autonomic nervous system arousal.

Unit: Micro-Siemens (µS) Sample Rate: 4 Hz
Blood Volume Pulse (PPG / HR) -- BPM

Optical photoplethysmography waveform for real-time heart rate (BPM) and heart rate variability (HRV SDNN).

Unit: Raw ADC / BPM Sample Rate: 64 Hz
Peripheral Skin Temperature -- °C

Monitors thermal deviation and peripheral vasoconstriction during acute stress episodes.

Unit: Celsius (°C) Sample Rate: 4 Hz
3-Axis Accelerometer (X, Y, Z) -- g

Measures physical movement to filter out physical activity from cognitive arousal.

Unit: G-Force ($g$) Sample Rate: 32 Hz

Monitored Facial Features

Continuous metrics computed across rolling 5-second windows:

Facial Rigidity (Laplacian Sharpness)

Measures local texture variance (`Laplacian variance`) across the facial region to detect localized muscle tension and rigidity.

Behavioral Motion (Frame Delta)

Calculates absolute pixel differences (`cv2.absdiff`) between consecutive frames to quantify physical agitation vs. sedentary calm.

Postural Leaning (Face Area Ratio)

Monitors bounding box area relative to the total frame to track forward leaning toward the screen during challenging tasks.

Gaze & Head Orientation

Tracks horizontal and vertical head center displacement (`face_center_x/y`) to detect nervous posture shifts.
